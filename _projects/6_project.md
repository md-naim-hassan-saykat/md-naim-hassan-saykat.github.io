<p class="lead mb-4">
The <strong>Bangladesh AI Policy &amp; Innovation Lab (BAPIL)</strong> is an independent research initiative that designs how artificial intelligence can support evidence-based policymaking in Bangladesh, in Bangla and English, under explicit safeguards. BAPIL is a proposal: no institution has been established and no funding has been approved.
</p>

<p class="mb-4">
The work has two stages. The <strong>White Paper (Version 1.0, July 2026)</strong> sets out the conceptual framework and a vision to 2041. The <strong>Technical Blueprint (Version 1.0, October 2026)</strong> turns it into a buildable plan, with a tested open-source reference implementation and a separate eleven-page Blueprint Summary.
</p>

<hr class="my-5">

<h2 class="h3 mb-4">What the Technical Blueprint specifies</h2>

<ul class="mb-4">
<li>An institutional design: a university-anchored lab under a government MoU, with a Steering Board, an independent Ethics and Risk Review Committee and a Technical Architecture Board.</li>
<li>A reference architecture of twelve open, replaceable components, hosted in Bangladesh.</li>
<li>A bilingual, citation-grounded search and answer design that cites official sources or abstains, and a planned open benchmark (BanglaPolicyQA).</li>
<li>Data rules (four data classes, public data first) designed for Bangladesh's 2026 data-protection and data-management laws.</li>
<li>An assurance system: risk tiers, lifecycle gates, model cards and a public transparency register, mapped to OECD, UNESCO, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act.</li>
<li>Six pilots, a preparatory Phase 0 and a 36-month delivery plan with an indicative budget, a logical framework, KPIs and a risk register.</li>
<li>A strategic roadmap for 2026 to 2041 and beyond, and six decisions requested from the authorities.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="h3 mb-4">Reference implementation</h2>

<p class="mb-4">
The repository contains a small, dependency-free Python implementation of the core ideas, released with the Blueprint as version 0.1.0: Bangla text normalisation, bilingual BM25 search, cited extractive answers with abstention, risk-tier assessment, an AI system register with a transparency export, evaluation metrics and a hash-chained audit log. It has 49 unit tests that run on GitHub Actions. It is a research prototype, not a production system.
</p>
