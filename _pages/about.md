I am an artificial intelligence researcher working on **medical image analysis, computer vision and explainable AI**, and on **AI governance for the public sector**. I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at **Université Paris-Saclay** and an independent researcher based in Bangladesh.

My research in medical imaging focuses on models that remain reliable beyond the data they were trained on: external evaluation on independent datasets, calibration and uncertainty, and visual explanations that clinicians can inspect. In parallel, I lead the **Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL)**, an independent research initiative that designs how AI can support evidence-based policymaking in Bangladesh safely and openly.

### Research interests

- **Medical image analysis**, in particular dermoscopic skin-lesion classification
- **Cross-dataset generalization and external evaluation** (for example, HAM10000 to ISIC 2019)
- **Explainable and trustworthy AI**: Grad-CAM, calibration, uncertainty estimation
- **Vision Transformers and CNN architectures**, and efficient models for resource-constrained settings
- **AI governance and policy intelligence**: responsible AI, risk assessment, bilingual (Bangla and English) information retrieval for public policy

### Selected work

- **Generalizable skin-lesion classification:** seven deep learning architectures and a probability-level ensemble, evaluated internally on HAM10000 and externally on ISIC 2019, with calibration, bootstrap confidence intervals and Grad-CAM. Revised manuscript under review. [Project](/projects/1_project/) · [Code](https://github.com/md-naim-hassan-saykat/skin-lesion-classification-ensemble-ham10000) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17390952)
- **BAPIL Technical Blueprint (2026):** a buildable plan, in English and Bangla, for an AI policy-intelligence capability in Bangladesh, with an institutional design, reference architecture, governance and assurance system, tested reference implementation and 36-month delivery plan. [Project](/projects/6_project/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23587901)
- **Information retrieval, generative models and NLP:** patent retrieval with cross-encoder re-ranking, CycleGAN image translation, a DRAW-style recurrent VAE and text emotion recognition. [All projects](/projects/)
