        <h5 class="card-title mb-2">
          Generalizable Ensemble Deep Learning for Skin Lesion Classification:<br>
          Internal and External Validation on HAM10000 and ISIC 2019
        </h5>

        <p class="text-muted mb-2">
          <strong>Status:</strong> Under review (2026)<br>
          <strong>Author:</strong> Md Naim Hassan Saykat
        </p>

        <p class="card-text">
          This manuscript proposes a robust multi-model ensemble framework for automated skin lesion classification.
          The study integrates CNNs, ResNet, DenseNet, EfficientNet, ConvNeXt, MobileNet, and Vision Transformers,
          and evaluates generalization through <strong>internal validation on HAM10000</strong> and
          <strong>external validation on ISIC 2019</strong>.
          Explainability is addressed using <strong>Grad-CAM</strong> and uncertainty-aware analysis.
        </p>

        <ul class="small text-muted mb-3">
          <li>Multi-model ensemble learning</li>
          <li>Internal & external validation protocol</li>
          <li>Explainable AI (Grad-CAM)</li>

## In Preparation

<div class="card shadow-sm border-0 mb-4">
  <div class="card-body">

    <h5 class="card-title mb-1">
      Edge-Ready Skin Lesion Classification with Efficient Deep Ensembles and
      Latency-Aware Design: A Web-Based Diagnostic Tool
    </h5>

    <p class="text-muted mb-2">
      Manuscript in preparation. Target submission: 2026–27 academic year
    </p>

    <p class="card-text">
      This work investigates <strong>edge-efficient and latency-aware deep learning</strong>
      for medical image classification, with a focus on deployable skin lesion diagnosis.
      Building on prior ensemble learning results on HAM10000 and ISIC 2019,
      the study integrates <strong>model compression, quantization, and inference benchmarking</strong>
      to achieve clinically meaningful performance under real-world computational constraints.
    </p>

    <p class="card-text mb-3">
      The proposed system combines <strong>efficient deep ensembles</strong> (CNN, ResNet, Vision Transformer)
      with <strong>Grad-CAM-based explainability</strong> and evaluates
      <strong>accuracy–latency trade-offs</strong> on edge-oriented platforms.
      A lightweight web-based diagnostic interface is developed to demonstrate
      practical deployment feasibility.
    </p>
