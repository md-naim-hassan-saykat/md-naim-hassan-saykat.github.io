        <h5 class="card-title mb-2">
          Generalizable Ensemble Deep Learning for Dermoscopic Skin-Lesion Classification:
          Internal Evaluation on HAM10000 and External Evaluation on ISIC 2019
        </h5>

        <p class="text-muted mb-3">
          <strong>Status:</strong> Under review (2026)<br>
          <strong>Author:</strong> Md Naim Hassan Saykat
        </p>

        <p class="card-text">
          This study investigates the generalizability of deep learning models for
          multiclass dermoscopic skin-lesion classification using harmonized internal
          evaluation on <strong>HAM10000</strong> and external evaluation on
          <strong>ISIC 2019</strong>.
        </p>

        <p class="card-text">
          Seven architectures are evaluated: a conventional CNN, ResNet-50,
          DenseNet-121, EfficientNet-B3, ConvNeXt-Tiny, MobileNetV3-Large, and
          ViT-B/16. Their class-probability outputs are additionally combined using
          an equal-weight soft-voting ensemble.
        </p>

        <p class="card-text">
          The evaluation includes classification and discrimination metrics,
          calibration analysis, bootstrap confidence intervals, paired statistical
          comparisons, and <strong>Grad-CAM</strong>-based qualitative
          interpretability analysis. The study emphasizes external generalization
          and the limitations of relying exclusively on internal performance.
        </p>

        <ul class="small text-muted mb-3">
          <li>Seven deep learning architectures and probability-level ensemble learning</li>
          <li>HAM10000 internal evaluation and ISIC 2019 external evaluation</li>
          <li>Model calibration and bootstrap uncertainty analysis</li>
          <li>Paired statistical model comparisons</li>
          <li>Grad-CAM-based interpretability analysis</li>
          <li>Reproducible medical-image classification workflow</li>
        </ul>

## In Preparation

<div class="card shadow-sm border-0 mb-4">

  <div class="card-body">

    <h5 class="card-title mb-2">
      Edge-Ready Skin Lesion Classification with Efficient Deep Ensembles and
      Latency-Aware Design: A Web-Based Decision-Support Prototype
    </h5>

    <p class="text-muted mb-3">
      <strong>Status:</strong> Research in preparation<br>
      <strong>Planned submission:</strong> 2026-27 academic year
    </p>

    <p class="card-text">
      This planned study extends the skin-lesion classification research toward
      computationally efficient deployment. It will investigate the trade-offs
      among predictive performance, model size, inference latency, and computational
      requirements for resource-constrained environments.
    </p>

    <p class="card-text">
      The planned methodology includes efficient model and ensemble configurations,
      model compression, quantization, inference benchmarking, and
      Grad-CAM-based interpretability. Evaluation will focus on the
      <strong>performance-efficiency trade-off</strong> rather than predictive
      performance alone.
    </p>

    <p class="card-text mb-3">
      A lightweight web-based research prototype is also planned to demonstrate
      model inference and visual explanation workflows. The prototype is intended
      for research and demonstration purposes and is not a clinical diagnostic
      system.
    </p>
